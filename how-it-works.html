Four steps, then the bag

There is no account and no app. The site, a till text, and the name on the bag are the whole trail.

  • 1. Read the menu

    Prices include packing. If a pot is empty the helpline says so. Sunday uses the same list at two counters only.

  • 2. Send the order

    Name, phone, counter, slot, plates. The kitchen will not start a bag with no phone number.

  • 3. Pay the till

    You receive a text from 400200. Pay that till, not a personal line. The bag is packed after the payment shows.

  • 4. Collect

    Arrive in your slot. Say the name. Take the bag. If you are more than thirty minutes late we call; after ninety minutes we cannot keep the rice.

What a late bag means

If the shelf does not have your name at the start of the slot, wait at the hatch and call 0711 400 200. Do not pay a second time. The kitchen will either finish the plate or refund the till in the same hour.

Kitchen notes

  • Slots run every hour from 11:30. Last slot 19:30.
  • Group orders: one form, several plates, one name on every bag.
  • Helpline: 0711 400 200.
