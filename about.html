A kitchen built for people who cannot sit down

Pick and Go started on Ngong Road in 2024. The first counter was a hatch beside a chemist. Office workers ordered by SMS, paid on M-Pesa, and collected a labelled bag. That hatch still runs. Four more counters followed because the same problem shows up all over Nairobi: you want a proper plate, you do not have forty minutes.

We are Team Odysseus. The public site is the front of the kitchen — menu, order, pickup times — so a visitor can finish an order without a phone call.

What we will not do

We do not run table service. We do not keep food under a lamp for an hour. An order is cooked for the slot you chose. If you miss the slot by more than thirty minutes, we hold the bag in a warmer and call the number on the form. After ninety minutes we cannot guarantee the rice.

How the kitchen is run

One menu is set every Sunday night. All five counters cook from it. Portions are weighed, not guessed. Beef, chicken and beans are cooked in the morning; chapati, ugali and rice are made through the day. Vegetarian plates are packed on a separate board.

If you have a nut or dairy note, write it on the order form. The kitchen reads every note before the bag is sealed.
