Campus dining at Strathmore, made easier

Pick and Go helps Strathmore University students discover campus restaurants, check menus and plan where to eat. Find a quick coffee between classes, a Swahili meal for lunch or a place to sit and enjoy a meal with friends.

Browse food spots by campus floor, see their menus and choose a pickup time that fits your day. The goal is simple: make it easier to find good food on campus and spend less time waiting in line.

Explore Strathmore food spots

Pick and Go brings the campus dining options together in one place, including the SU Main Coffee Station on the ground floor, Swahili dishes and burgers on the first floor, and the Grand Dining Hall on the top floor.

Whether you are grabbing a drink on the way to class or choosing a full meal, you can compare your options before heading to the restaurant.

Made for student life

Between lectures, study sessions and campus activities, every minute counts. Browse restaurant choices, plan your order and pick a convenient time so lunch fits around your schedule.

Pick and Go is a student-focused campus dining project by Team Odysseus, created to make finding and ordering food at Strathmore simpler.
