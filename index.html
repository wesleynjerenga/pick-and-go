Food ready when you are

Pick and Go is a Nairobi pickup kitchen. You place an order, choose a counter, and collect a bag with your name on it. We cook to the slot you pick, so lunch does not mean twenty minutes on a pavement.

The kitchen runs five counters: Westlands, Kenyatta Avenue, Ngong Road, Rongai and Kiserian. The same menu, the same prices, one payment on the order form.
